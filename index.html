How to fight

Tap
Strike. Fast, light damage, builds energy.
Swipe
Slash left or right. Heavier hit.
Swipe up
Special, once energy is full. Breaks an enemy wind-up.
Swipe down
Parry. When an enemy winds up, a red ring closes on your beast. Swipe down once it turns gold to block the hit and counter.
Portraits
Tap one to swap beasts mid-fight.
